Crucial Red Flags in the Kenyan Tech Job Market:

1. Upfront "Interview / Medical" M-Pesa Fees: Under Kenyan Employment Act 2007, an employer or agency cannot charge any fee to a job seeker. Any M-Pesa request is 100% a fraud.
2. "Free Laptop Shipping" Scam: Fraudsters claim you got a remote developer job but ask you to pay KES 2,500 - KES 6,000 courier delivery fee for a company MacBook.
3. Free Email Domains for MNCs: Recruiters claiming to represent Safaricom, Microsoft, or Equity writing from `@gmail.com` or `@yahoo.com` instead of official corporate domains.
4. Unpaid Exploitative Attachments: Industrial attachments in Kenya should provide academic supervision, NITA logbook signing, and where possible, basic transport stipends.

Local vs Worldwide Remote Hiring Mechanics for Kenyans

Kenyan software engineers have two primary pathways in today's global work pool:

1. Kenyan Local Entity (PAYE / Statutory): Full-time Kenyan contract with Safaricom, Equity, KCB, Microsoft ADC, or local scaleups. Benefits include NHIF/SHIF, NSSF, Housing Levy deduction, and Kenyan Sacco linkages.
2. Worldwide Remote via EOR / W-8BEN: Hired by US/EU firms (GitLab, Canonical, Automattic, Turing, Deel). Remuneration received via wire transfer or Deel/Payoneer/M-Pesa in USD. Tax filed via KRA iTax turnover/income tax self-assessment.
Pro Tip: Kenyan engineers who master Go, Python, or TypeScript alongside cloud automation command on average 2.4x higher compensation in remote international pools.
